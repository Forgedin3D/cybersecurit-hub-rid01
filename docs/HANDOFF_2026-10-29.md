## 4. Firmware/UI truth rules (do not regress)

1. **Passive cooperative Remote ID reception**, not conventional radar or non-cooperative detection. Current firmware listens for **BLE**, not Wi-Fi RID.
2. Multi-target backend: maximum **T01–T04**, targets associated by advertiser identity, independently ACTIVE/STALE/LOST. Allocated slot number depends on arrival order; do not assume R111S always T01.
3. Prior lifecycle reference thresholds: ACTIVE through about **3,000 ms**, STALE through about **10,000 ms**, LOST after that; reuse eligibility after about **15,000 ms**. Do not change them accidentally during UI/radio work.
4. Operator-owned continuous scanning uses asynchronous BLE windows (around 60 s rollover). Session stays active until intentional STOP/RESCAN; rollover should not drop target state or snapshots.
5. **RADAR is geographic, north-up and coordinate-derived**: valid receiver reference + fresh target geographic coordinates required. Use great-circle horizontal separation and true initial bearing; **never convert RSSI to distance/bearing**. No GNSS fix or invalid receiver reference must not prevent **scan-only TARGETS detection**.
6. Fresh in-range geographic markers are cyan; off-scale markers yellow. Never-allocated slots hidden; allocated targets lacking fresh geographic data show **NO FRESH GEO** in panel; stale positions **must not plot**; LOST hidden. Right-panel ACTIVE green, STALE yellow.
7. PRIVATE is default; OPEN deliberately selected for controlled local detail. SNAPSHOT is frozen, respects privacy mode; RESCAN resets capture generation. Global MODE control exists on RADAR and does not affect geographic plotting merely through privacy selection.
8. **D4G STOP/HOME:** If scan-stop request fails, do not falsely return HOME. Normal successful STOP returns HOME and does not display acknowledgment again.
9. **D4H cold-power-on notice:** Informational disclaimer before controls start scanning. Full power-off/on -> notice. RESET before acknowledgment -> notice still required. RESET after acknowledgment -> HOME. STOP/HOME -> HOME without repeat. D4H uses RTC retained magic/check state on supported warm resets; reset reasons and RTC retention remain hardware-specific, tested on this board.
10. Receiver reference currently manual test via `R,<lat>,<lon>`, `R,STATUS`, `R,CLEAR`, RAM-only. Never paste or commit actual private coordinates. Future LC76G should replace reference provider without rewriting core solver.

**On-device D4H notice:** Heading `RID-01 OPERATOR NOTICE`; text states it receives broadcast Remote ID, signal availability/position accuracy vary, data may be delayed/incomplete/spoofed, informational only and not certified collision-avoidance; do not rely on it alone for flight safety. Button: `ACKNOWLEDGE & CONTINUE`. It is not a liability waiver or legal certification.

## 6. Hardware and immediate LiPo safety gate

**Waveshare Rev2.0:** 1S 3.7 V battery connector labeled **BAT**, miniature **MX1.25 2-pin**, AXP2101 power management/charging, USB-C, expansion header, optional external-antenna IPEX path requiring microscopic SMD resistor rework. Two boards owned, one spare. Do not make antenna changes without measured RF benefit and practice; avoid modifications to the only accepted board.

**Received candidate battery:** Sold under Amazon ASIN **B0FPCWFFYB** through YELUFT; MakerFocus-branded 3.7 V **2000 mAh / 7.4 Wh** single-cell LiPo with small visible protection board, 2-pin white plug and yellow protective insulating tape. Four-pack purchased. **Nominal voltage/capacity appear suitable, but actual voltage, polarity, exact mating/keying, protection function, and charger compatibility are NOT proven.** Pack's recommended current was discussed as ~400 mA, with 4.2 V single-cell maximum, but verify **the exact model datasheet and Waveshare/firmware AXP2101 settings** before charging. A seller's connector-name claim (JST/SH vs MX1.25) or red/black wire colors are **not proof of compatibility**.

**Received photos:** Multiple macro photographs of pack, contacts, PCB BAT markings, wire orientation and protection circuit through tape. Yellow/amber tape is electrical insulation **and must not be peeled, cut or disturbed**. Never pierce wire jackets, probe underneath tape, crush or bend pouch, or short connector contacts.

**Measurement tools ordered Oct 29:** **KAIWEETS KM100** multimeter (DC volts / continuity); separate **0.7 mm fine probe tips** expected later the same day. Use **COM** and **VΩmA**, select DC volts around the **20 V** range. Keep LiPo disconnected from the board. Only contact its two connector terminals with independently isolated fine probes; if probes cannot safely contact without bridging, **stop instead of improvising a short**. Never place meter in 10A/current mode across pack. Never test resistance/continuity across live pack output.

**Battery acceptance order:**

1. Inspect undamaged/unswollen pouch resting on a nonflammable, nonconductive surface; do not charge a questionable pack.
2. Meter check and safe DC measurement of battery connector **voltage and polarity** (including positive/negative sign and orientation/photo).
3. Read BAT+ / BAT- silkscreen and verified Rev2.0 socket pinout; confirm connector **pitch, fit, orientation and mating polarity**. Do not force insertion.
4. Review installed sketch for actual AXP2101 charger initialization/settings; verify pack's permitted charging voltage/current, not just PMIC default assumptions.
5. Only if all prior gates pass: controlled battery-only startup, cold-boot D4H notice, measured runtime and separately supervised USB-C charging. No parallel packs.
6. If connector cannot safely mate, polarity reverses, or electrical readings are abnormal, **do not connect**. Decide whether existing parts can safely address issue or return the batteries—no unnecessary accessory purchases.

**User's hard stop:** Determine whether these batteries are compatible or must be returned; do not prolong the hardware task with needless purchases. If a safe test cannot be done with present tools, say what remains unverified rather than override the safety gate.
