## Privacy and interpretation

EXIF/GPS/XMP checks were repeated for the selected JPEGs. Video probes confirm one video stream and no original location/time/audio/auxiliary tracks. Ordinary codec metadata remains. No source image is repainted as a simulated screen: only recorded crops, re-encoding and disclosed opaque privacy masks are used. Relative distance/bearing, target-slot labels and ordinary reflections remain; owner review is required before publication.

[MEDIA_MANIFEST.json](MEDIA_MANIFEST.json) records original and sharing-copy hashes/crops. Raw GPS metadata values and real RID identifiers are not transcribed. Camera dates are chronology clues, not independent authentication.
